GROWING KNOW-HOW

Little tips. Healthier plants.

FIELD
NOTES / 01
01
☼

Know the light

Check a plant’s light needs before choosing its spot. Bright indirect light suits many indoor plants, while others prefer direct sun.

02
⌁

Water with purpose

Check the soil before watering. Pots need drainage, and watering frequency changes with weather, plant type, and pot size.

03
✿

Start with healthy soil

Choose a growing medium suited to the plant, and avoid leaving roots sitting in water. Follow product labels when using soil amendments.
